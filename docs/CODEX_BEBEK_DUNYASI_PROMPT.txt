You are generating the complete art asset pack for "Our Baby World", a cozy virtual-pet game inside a couples' chat app. Two partners raise one child together: they walk the child around a little house and outdoors, bathe, feed, dress and play with them. Your job in this task is ONLY to create image files (PNG) exactly as specified below, plus one manifest file. Do NOT modify, create or delete any source code, styles or configuration in the repository. Another engineer is writing the game code against the exact file names, sizes and anchors in this document, so follow them precisely. If an image cannot be produced perfectly, still produce the closest possible version with the exact file name and canvas size, and list it under "warnings" in the manifest.

====================================================================
1. OUTPUT LOCATION AND FORMAT
====================================================================
- Root folder: client/public/baby/world/
- Every file is PNG. "Opaque" means a fully painted background. "Transparent" means a real alpha channel with a fully transparent background (no white, no checkerboard pattern painted in, no magenta).
- Never put text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures, UI labels or watermarks inside any image.
- Never crop a subject at the canvas edge unless this document says the subject is part of a full background.
- When everything is done, write client/public/baby/world/manifest.json listing every file you created as { "path": "...", "width": n, "height": n, "transparent": true|false } plus a "warnings" array (empty if none).

====================================================================
2. ART STYLE BIBLE (applies to EVERY image)
====================================================================
Look: a premium, cozy mobile pet game with a soft 3D cartoon look, like smooth vinyl toys or polished clay figures rendered in a modern 3D cartoon film style. Rounded, chunky, friendly shapes; no sharp corners anywhere. Everything should feel touchable and squishy.
- Lighting: soft warm key light from the TOP-LEFT on every single asset, soft fill light from the right, gentle ambient occlusion in creases, subtle rim light on characters. Lighting direction must be identical across all files so pieces combine into one believable scene.
- Materials: matte to satin plastics, soft fabrics with visible but gentle fold volume, warm wood with smooth rounded edges, glossy ceramics in the bathroom, shiny water.
- Color: warm pastel palette with clear contrast: peach, cream, butter yellow, mint, sky blue, lavender, coral pink, warm wood browns. Avoid neon. Avoid gray, muddy or dark-heavy images (night images are dark blue and cozy, never black).
- Depth: backgrounds use real perspective (see camera rules) with atmospheric depth: things farther away are slightly lighter and less saturated.
- Shadows: transparent assets have NO cast shadow on the ground (the game adds shadows), but DO have their own soft self-shading and ambient occlusion so they look 3D.
- Consistency: every character, prop and room must look like it belongs to the same game, made by the same studio, on the same day.

====================================================================
3. CAMERA RULES FOR SCENES (critical for the game to work)
====================================================================
All scene backgrounds share ONE camera:
- Front view, camera at the eye height of a small child, tilted very slightly down, so the floor is clearly visible and walls rise behind it.
- Canvas 3072 x 1536 (wide 2:1 panorama; the game pans the camera left and right as the child walks).
- The line where the back wall meets the floor (or the horizon outdoors) is a straight horizontal line at exactly 56% of the image height (y = 860).
- The WALKING BAND is the floor area from y = 1000 to y = 1460, across the full width. Keep this band completely free of furniture, objects, rugs with strong patterns, or anything a child could look like they are standing inside. Floors there must be plain and calm (wood planks, tiles, grass, path) so a character can stand anywhere on it.
- Furniture and decorations in the background stand against the back wall (bottom edges between y = 860 and y = 1000) or at the far left/right ends of the panorama.
- Leave the left 10% and right 10% of the image as natural "ends" of the room (a wall corner, a door, a big plant, a fence) so panning never reveals emptiness.
- Scale reference: a door in a back wall is 620 px tall. A 10-year-old child standing on the walking band is about 700 px tall.
